**Weak harness.** `verify_c1_preimage_bound` counts matches among 9 neighbouring
divisors and asserts the count is at most 9, so the assertion cannot fail. Its
"zero preimages" cover is unsatisfiable, because the first value compared is the
input itself. Its success shows no useful preimage bound.

It was rewritten after this run: it now asserts that each divisor in
`[2^36, 2^36 + 1024]` gives a strictly larger quotient than the next one. The new
version was run individually: SUCCESSFUL (45 s, both covers satisfied), and
FAILED with the division result deliberately truncated. No full run has been made
since the rewrite.

## Replaced harnesses

`verify_c3_step_non_injective` and `verify_c3_two_step_preimage_growth` ran a full
`verlet_step` on symbolic positions. Neither produced a verdict (more than 22 hours,
and the 1-hour limit, respectively), and both are replaced on `dev`. The
replacements are **narrower**: they check division rounding on a few concrete
denominators, not non-injectivity of the Verlet step.
